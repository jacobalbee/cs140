St. Vitus Cathedral & Prague Castle

One of the most magnificent places that my wife and I visited when we arrived in Prague was the historic Prague Castle, at the heart of which lies the famous St. Vitus Cathedral.

The Castle is the largest ancient castle complex in the world, according to the Guinness Book of World Records, and was founded in 880 AD by Prince Borivoj, the first Christian prince of Bohemia, as a wooden fortress. The first walled building constructed at the site was the Church of the Virgin Mary, but other churches, dedicated to St. George and St. Vitus were built in the 10th century.
