Summary

From November 2021 to November 2023, I served as a missionary for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the California San Bernardino Mission. I was set apart as a missionary on October 31st (Halloween Day), began online MTC the following week, and then flew to the Missionary Training Center (MTC) in Provo, Utah. After a short period of missionary preparation and training, I flew from Salt Lake City, Utah to Ontario, California, where I was met by my first mission president, President McKinnon. I was assigned to the Brentwood Ward and Mojave Samoan Ward in Victorville and Adelanto, CA, serving alongside my mission trainer Elder Carson Diederich, from Highland, Utah. Adelanto and Victorville sit within the High (Mojave) Desert region of Southern California: an expansive desert known for its ubiquitous Joshua trees (named "Joshua trees" initially by the Latter-day Saint pioneers that settled San Bernardino, who noted their resemblance to the hands of the biblical Joshua, ushering them into the promised land). After one transfer, I was assigned to the Mojave Narrows Ward in Apple Valley, CA with Elder Connor Elliott from Boise, Idaho. I served in that ward for two transfers, the second tranfer serving alongside Elder Jared Simper from Lewiston, Utah. In the Mojave Narrows Ward, I and my companions baptized Norbert Murrieta. Since this point, his adult children and his mother have all been baptized into the faith. We also baptized Brody, Kaysee, and Evan Jones, three children, all over the age of eight, being raised by their Latter-day Saint grandmother, who had just recently returned to church. After two transfers in Apple Valley, I was moved to Hesperia, CA, where I served for two additional transfers in the Arrowhead Wardwith Elder Samuel Scott from Gilbert, Arizona.

Finally, I was transferred to serve in the Baseline Ward of Fontana, CA with Elder Bridger Jones from Kaysville, Utah. The cities of Apple Valley, Victorville, and Hesperia all border one another in the High Desert. Fontana was my first area outside the High Desert, and inside the very urban San Bernardino Valley, filled with palm trees. I served with Elder Jones for two transfers, and then with Elder Bryson Jensen from Idaho Falls, Idaho for one transfer. We helped reactivate several member families in the Fontana area, and baptized multiple children from these families: Olaoluwaju ("Sean") Shittu, Levi Brady, Natalie Brady, and Brandon Gutierrez. After my time in Fontana, I was moved to the urban center of the mission: San Bernardino, CA: a large city founded by Latter-day Saints in the 1800s who were subsequently recalled by Brigham Young when tensions with the United States government peaked during the Utah War. There, I served in the Waterman Ward with Elder Uasila'a ("Christian") Lauaki from Independence, Missouri for one transfer, at the end of which he went home, finishing his mission. For my second transfer in the area, I served with Elder Brandon Braby from Pleasant Grove, Utah. Within that transfer, we began teaching a young couple: Donovan Barela and Kacey White. They loved the Book of Mormon and, while they were not baptized during my time in San Bernardino, they were married and were baptized before I returned home.

After this final transfer in San Bernardino, I was moved out to a semi-abandoned town in the Low (Sonoran) Desert Region, south of Palm Springs. The town, Salton City, was built on the shores of an accidental artificial lake, which eventually became so polluted and salty that virtually nothing could live in it anymore. Once the lake became unsuitable for most fish life, most residents abandoned the town, leaving empty streets and abandoned homes. This area was one of the best in my entire mission. My companion for the first two transfers was Elder Logan Taylor from Shelley, Idaho. For my last tranfer there, my companion was Elder Nicholas Anderson from Reno, Nevada. During my three transfers in the Borrego Springs Branch, I saw the baptisms of individuals: Clifford Counter, Elaine Smith, Elias Rosales, Ellie Rosales, Deanei, Glenn, Gill, and Rhoda Wright, Myeshia and Richawn Lewis, Maricela Acuna, Kalia Martinez, Isaac Contreras, Zachery Carter, and Regina Ruthosky. We watched that tiny branch blossom and grow. I left that area sad but satisfied with the change that had taken place. For my final area, I was sent to the borders of the San Bernardino Valley and the Low Desert: to a city called Banning, with my companion Hyrum Pectol from Tooele, Utah. Elder Pectol and I worked together for only two weeks before he was suddenly transferred to another area. I finished that transfer with Elder Jeffrey Rumsey from Clinton, Utah, with whom I served for four weeks. The next transfer was my second-to-last. I was chosen to train a brand new missionary, Elder Aaron Bradshaw from Burley, Idaho. Elder Bradshaw and I served together for two transfers in the Banning Ward. In the final week of my final transfer, we baptized Roy Gregory. I finished my mission after two years of service. I learned so much from the experience: the value of hard work, the importance of moral standards, the depravity of sin, the power of the Atonement of Jesus Christ, and the joy of the restored gospel.
